Die status cues

Three ways to show what's going on with a die, all on the real 50px tile from DieCube.tsx (same gradient, corners, damage badge and picked ring). Tap any die to open the explainer at the bottom of its column.

Shared by all three
Amber: combat (block / attack rules). Not an energy colour, so "Unblockable" stops reading as Eye.
Cream: abilities (blanked, granted keyword, lockout).
Red: will be KO'd, same family as the damage badge.
Filled = must / has. Outlined + slash = can't / off. Each cue also has a glyph or word, so colour is never the only signal.
Permanent keywords: no tile marker

Mark changes, not identity. The animal icon already tells you the card, and keywords are printed on it, so a marker on every tile would mean every die carries a badge. Then the cues that really matter (a forced block, a pending KO) stop standing out. Keywords stay in the tap explainer. A granted keyword gets a cue because it's a change.

After you pick one: desktop light/dark, the unblockable-lane treatment, the spin-down flash in motion, and a first-time legend.
